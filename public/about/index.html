About Telar Labs

Telar Labs is a small, independent studio that builds simple, free web tools for teachers, students and content creators.

We started with a simple observation: many classrooms have a projector or TV but no expensive interactive board, many online quizzes are easy to cheat on, and creators everywhere keep needing the same holiday and greeting backgrounds. Good tools exist, but they often need sign-ups, subscriptions or fast internet. We try to make tools that just open and work.

What we make

  • Telar Clips: Free motion backgrounds, illustrated scenes and green screens for greetings, reels, intros and school or church videos. clips.telarlabs.net
  • PizarraPad: A free online whiteboard, blackboard and greenboard for the classroom projector. Write with a stylus, finger or mouse, add pictures, works offline. pizarrapad.telarlabs.net
  • QuizPolice: Free quiz monitoring for Google Forms, Microsoft Forms and Wayground. See live who left the quiz tab, and for how long. quizpolice.telarlabs.net
  • DraftTrail: A writing recorder that replays how an essay was written, step by step, so teachers can see the process, not just the result. drafttrail.telarlabs.net
  • Telar Stories: Turn illustrated scenes and characters into 2 to 3 minute narrated story videos for classrooms, Sunday school and organization promos.

How we work

  • Free versions with no sign-up.
  • Data stays on your device wherever possible.
  • No ads inside the tools while you teach or create; information pages show a few ads to pay for hosting.
  • We use AI assistance in parts of our design and illustration work, and review everything we publish.
