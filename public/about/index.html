About Telar Labs

Telar Labs is a small, independent studio that builds simple web tools for teachers, students, event organizers and content creators. Most of what we make is free, works on any phone or laptop, and opens without an account.

Why we build these tools

Our apps start from everyday problems we keep hearing about from schools, churches, clubs and small teams:

  • Classrooms often have a projector or TV but no interactive board, and the internet isn't always reliable. Online quizzes are easy to cheat on, and AI has made it harder to know how an essay was written.
  • Small organizers running reunions, fun runs, fitness classes or church concerts end up juggling group chats, spreadsheets and payment screenshots, while big ticketing platforms take a cut of every ticket.
  • Creators and volunteers who make greeting, school and church videos keep needing the same backgrounds, overlays and holiday clips, in the right sizes for each platform.

Good tools exist for all of this, but many need sign-ups, subscriptions, fast internet or a big budget. We try to make tools that just open and work.

What we make

  • Telar Clips: Free motion backgrounds, illustrated scenes and green screens for greetings, reels, intros and school or church videos. clips.telarlabs.net
  • PizarraPad: A free online whiteboard, blackboard and greenboard for the classroom projector. Write with a stylus, finger or mouse, add pictures, works offline. pizarrapad.telarlabs.net
  • QuizPolice: Free quiz monitoring for Google Forms, Microsoft Forms and Wayground. See live who left the quiz tab, and for how long. quizpolice.telarlabs.net
  • DraftTrail: A writing recorder that replays how an essay was written, step by step, so teachers can see the process, not just the result. drafttrail.telarlabs.net
  • Tixward: Sell event tickets and take bookings for courts and rooms. Buyers pay you directly by e-wallet, bank QR, card link or cash, and get QR tickets. Free for most events. tixward.telarlabs.net
  • Telar Stories: Turn illustrated scenes and characters into 2 to 3 minute narrated story videos for classrooms, Sunday school, organization promos, and parents making simple custom stories for their children.

What we promise

  • Free to start, no sign-up. Teachers and students don't need accounts. Organizers get a private link instead of a password.
  • Your data stays small and short-lived. PizarraPad saves boards in your own browser. DraftTrail recordings stay on the student's device until they hand in the file. QuizPolice deletes quiz data 30 days after a quiz is created. Tixward removes payment screenshots 30 days after an event and the rest of the event data after 90 days.
  • Money goes where it should. On Tixward, buyers pay organizers directly. We never take a percentage of ticket sales.
  • No ads inside the tools. Students taking a quiz, teachers at the board and buyers paying for tickets never see ads. Some information pages and guides may show a few ads to help pay for hosting.
  • Built for ordinary devices. We test on phones as well as laptops, and on slow connections. Where we can, tools keep working offline.
  • Honest about limits. No monitor catches every kind of cheating, and no recorder can prove who wrote something. We say so in our apps and guides.

How we're funded

We keep the core tools free. Hosting and development are paid for by a small number of ads on information pages and by Tixward's one-time fee for larger events (events with up to 300 paid tickets are free, and free tickets are always free). Telar Clips are free to download and use.

How we make things

  • Every app is designed, built and tested by the Telar Labs team, then improved based on feedback from the people using it.
  • We use AI assistance in parts of our design, illustration and writing work. Everything is reviewed by the team before it's published.
  • Our motion graphics are original: generated in code or illustrated for Telar Labs, never copied from other libraries.
  • Screenshots in our guides use sample data with made-up names, never real users' information.

Our guides

Alongside the apps, we publish practical guides for teachers, event organizers and creators: how to reduce cheating in online quizzes, teach with a projector, sell tickets when buyers pay you directly, plan a reunion, or size a video for each platform. Each guide comes from the problems people bring to us while using our tools.

  • Guides are written and reviewed by the Telar Labs team, and dated so you know how current they are.
  • We test the steps ourselves, and we're open about the limits of every tool, including our own.
  • When a guide mentions one of our apps, we say so plainly. The advice works with other tools too.
  • When a setting or price changes, we update the guide. If you spot something out of date, please tell us.
