Free to use

Every app has a free version with no account needed. The information pages show a few ads to cover hosting; the tools themselves stay clean while you teach or create.

Your data stays with you

Boards, drafts and quiz keys are saved in your own browser. We collect as little as possible, and quiz data is deleted automatically after 30 days.

Built for real classrooms

Big buttons, works on phones and old laptops, keeps working when the Wi-Fi drops, and available in English, Spanish and Chinese where it matters.
