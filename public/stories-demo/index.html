Make a story video in 4 steps

A sample story is already loaded so you can see how it works. Everything in it can be changed.

  1. Pick a scene in the strip under the picture. Add more with + Scene.
  2. Set the scene: open Background to choose a place and Characters to add poses. Drag characters to move them; tap one to flip, resize or remove it.
  3. Tell the story: in Words & voice, type what happens and press Record my voice to read it aloud. The title and words in the sample are just suggestions; mix and match.
  4. Press ▶ Play to watch it, then ⬇ Make video to save it to your device.

Making your video…

Keep this tab open. The video is recorded in real time, so it takes as long as the story.
