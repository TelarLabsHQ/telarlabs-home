Story Order

Each story has its own Story Order game. Children see four pictures from the story, mixed up, and tap them in the order they happened. When they finish, the pictures line up with a short sentence for each one, ready for retelling.

Why sequencing matters

Putting events in order is one of the first reading comprehension skills. It helps children understand that stories have a beginning, a middle and an end, and gives them words like first, next, then and last. Retelling a story in order is also great speaking practice.

Playing together

These games are made to be played with a grown-up nearby. Talk about the pictures, ask questions and celebrate together. For more ideas, each story page has questions to talk about and a free printable activity pack.
