Memory Match

Turn over two cards at a time and find the matching characters.

Moves: 0
🎉 You found them all!

How to play

  1. Choose Easy (6 pairs), Medium (8 pairs) or Hard (12 pairs).
  2. Tap a card to turn it over, then tap another one.
  3. If the two cards show the same character, they stay open. If not, they turn back over. Try to remember where each one was!
  4. Find all the pairs in as few moves as you can.

The 🔊 button turns the sounds on or off.

Meet the characters

Every card is a character from Telar Stories: Mira and her cat Mochi, Mei, Leo and Grandma, Biscuit the dog and Pip the bird, Mr. Tan the teacher, Lily from The Christmas Gift, Noah from Noah’s Ark, the shepherd boy from The First Christmas and Mary’s donkey. Children who know the stories love finding their favorites.

What memory games help with

  • Working memory: holding where each card was while looking for its pair.
  • Focus and patience: slowing down and remembering instead of tapping at random.
  • Language: say each character’s name out loud when the card turns over. Younger children can practice naming, older ones can read the name under the picture.
  • Taking turns: play together, one card pair each, and count who finds more.

Tips for parents and teachers

  • Start with Easy for ages 3 to 5, and move up when your child finishes with three stars.
  • Talk about the characters: “Where did we see Mochi? What did Lily give Mr. Ben?”
  • After a round, open a story with the same characters in Telar Stories and make a short video together.
  • On a classroom screen, let children come up one at a time to choose two cards.

The game is free, needs no sign-up and works on phones, tablets and computers. Nothing is saved except the best score on this device.
