The Christmas Gift

Ages 4 to 9About 2 minutesKindness, giving, thinking of others

A free narrated picture-book story about Lily, who gives up the gift she wanted to cheer up her lonely neighbor, with a printable coloring page, sequencing sheet and discussion questions.

▶ Open this story in Telar Stories

The story

All year, Lily has saved her coins to buy a snow globe with a tiny dancing bear. On the way home from the toy shop, she sees her old neighbor, Mr. Ben, sitting alone by his window. Mom explains that his family lives far away. Lily thinks about the snow globe, then about Mr. Ben, and has an idea. She spends her coins on flour and sprinkles, bakes cookies with Mom, and on Christmas Eve brings them to Mr. Ben with a card. He smiles the biggest smile, and they sing Christmas songs together.

What it teaches

The best gifts are the ones we give. Lily chooses kindness over something she wanted for herself, and discovers it makes her happier. It’s a gentle way to talk with children about noticing lonely people, and about giving time and care, not only things.

    Before the story

    • What would you like for Christmas?
    • Do you know someone who might feel lonely at Christmas?
    • What makes a gift special?

    After the story: questions to talk about

    • What was Lily saving her coins for?
    • Why was Mr. Ben alone?
    • What did Lily decide to do with her coins? Why?
    • How did Mr. Ben feel when he got the cookies?
    • How did Lily feel at the end?
    • Who could you make happy with a small gift or a visit?

    Activities to try

    • Kindness cookies: bake or decorate cookies together and give them to a neighbor, a grandparent or a school guard.
    • Card for someone lonely: make a Christmas card for someone who lives alone or far from family.
    • Coin jar: start a family jar for helping others, and decide together how to use it.
    • Make it yours: in Telar Stories, change Lily’s name and Mr. Ben’s name to people your child knows, and record a message at the end.
