Noah’s Ark

Ages 3 to 8About 2 minutesTrusting and obeying God, God’s promise

A free narrated picture-book telling of Noah’s Ark for young children, with the animals coming two by two, the great rain and the dove with the olive leaf, plus a printable coloring page, sequencing sheet and discussion questions.

▶ Open this story in Telar Stories

The story

Long ago, many people forgot to be kind and forgot about God, but Noah loved God and always tried to do what was right. God told Noah to build a very big boat called an ark. Noah and his family worked hard, day after day, and then the animals came, two by two: lions and elephants, giraffes and zebras, sheep and rabbits. The rain fell for forty days and forty nights, but inside the ark everyone was safe. At last the rain stopped, and a dove came back with an olive leaf. The land was drying!

What it teaches

Noah trusted God even when building a huge boat on dry land seemed strange. The story shows that God takes care of those who trust Him, and the rainbow is a sign of God’s promise.

Before the story

  • What is your favorite animal?
  • Have you ever been caught in a big rainstorm? How did it feel?
  • What do you think an ark is?

After the story: questions to talk about

  • Why did God choose Noah?
  • What did God ask Noah to build?
  • How did the animals come into the ark?
  • How long did it rain?
  • What did the dove bring back?
  • What does a rainbow remind us of?

Activities to try

  • Two by two: make pairs of animal cards and play a matching game.
  • Animal parade: children act like their favorite animal and march “into the ark” in pairs.
  • Rainbow promise: make a paper rainbow and write a promise on each color.
  • Count the animals: pause the story at the animals scene and count every pair together.
