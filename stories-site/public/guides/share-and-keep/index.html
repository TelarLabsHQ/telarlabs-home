Sharing your video and keeping your work

Telar Stories works entirely in your browser. That keeps your stories private, but it also means a few things are worth knowing.

Where your work is saved

  • Your story, pictures and voices are saved in this browser on this device. Close the page and come back later, and it's still there.
  • It isn't saved online, so it won't appear on your other devices.
  • Clearing your browser's data, using a private window, or a phone freeing up space can remove it.

Keep your finished video. Once you're happy with a story, press Make video and download it. The video file is yours to keep, even if the browser's data is cleared.

Sharing

  • Group chats: send the downloaded video in Messenger, WhatsApp, Viber or Telegram.
  • Classroom or church: play it on a TV or projector from a laptop or USB stick.
  • YouTube and Facebook: upload it like any other video. Stories are 16:9, so they fit YouTube and TVs.
  • Editing apps: open the MP4 in CapCut or a similar app to add your own music or intro.

Music

Under Whole story, choose background music: Gentle, Happy, Adventure, Dreamy, your own music file, or none. If you use your own music, make sure you're allowed to use it, especially on YouTube and Facebook.

Respect and privacy

  • Use children's real names only when the family is happy for the video to be shared.
  • For public posts, consider using a first name only, or a made-up name.
