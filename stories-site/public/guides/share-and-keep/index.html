Guides

Sharing your video and keeping your work

Telar Stories works entirely in your browser. That keeps your stories private, but it also means a few things are worth knowing.

Where your work is saved

  • Your story, pictures and voices are saved in this browser on this device. Close the page and come back later, and it's still there.
  • It isn't saved online, so it won't appear on your other devices by itself.
  • Clearing your browser's data, using a private window, or a phone freeing up space can remove it.

Save a backup

Under Keep your story safe, tap Save a backup. You get one file ending in telar-story-backup.json with the whole story: every scene, its picture and characters, the voices, the music choice and your pronunciation list. Keep it in Google Drive, email it to yourself, or copy it to a USB stick.

To continue on another phone or computer, open Telar Stories there, tap Open a backup and choose the file. The app asks before replacing the story on screen.

  • Don't open the backup file by itself; it only shows computer code. Always open it from Telar Stories.
  • If you used your own music file, choose it again after opening the backup.

Keep your finished video. Once you're happy with a story, press Make video and download it. The video file is yours to keep, even if the browser's data is cleared.

Sharing

  • Group chats: send the downloaded video in Messenger, WhatsApp, Viber or Telegram.
  • Classroom or church: play it on a TV or projector from a laptop or USB stick.
  • YouTube and Facebook: upload it like any other video. Stories are 16:9, so they fit YouTube and TVs.
  • Editing apps: open the MP4 in CapCut or a similar app to add your own music or intro.

Music

Under Whole story, choose background music: Gentle: Soft piano, Warm and happy: Picnic or Sunday, Easy and happy: Playful or Garden, your own music file, or no music. Our music is made for Telar Stories and free to use in your videos. If you use your own music, make sure you're allowed to use it, especially on YouTube and Facebook.

Respect and privacy

  • Use children's real names only when the family is happy for the video to be shared.
  • For public posts, consider using a first name only, or a made-up name.

Showing it to a group

  • On a TV or projector, play the downloaded MP4 file rather than the app itself. The video plays smoothly and doesn't need the internet.
  • Check the sound before the class or service. Narration is easier to hear through a speaker than from a laptop.
  • Pause and ask questions. Stop after a scene and ask, "What do you think happens next?"

Internet and data

The first visit downloads the pictures you use, and the first AI voice downloads about 90 MB. After that, much of it loads from your device. Use Wi-Fi for the first AI voice if you can. Playing and saving your video doesn't use the internet.
