Make your first story video in five minutes

You don't need to write or draw anything to get started. Every ready-made story already has pictures, words, gentle movements and a narrator's voice.

1. Pick a story

At the top of the page, under Ready-made stories, tap a story such as Mira and the Rainy Day or The Lost Sheep. If you've already changed the story on screen, the app asks before replacing it.

2. Watch it

Press ▶ Play at the top. The story plays scene by scene, with the narrator reading the words. The strip of small pictures under the main picture shows every scene. Tap one to jump to it.

3. Save the video

Press ⬇ Make video. A progress bar and a percentage show how far along it is. Keep the screen open while the video is made: if you switch apps or lock your phone, it pauses and continues when you come back.

When it reaches 100%, the video is saved automatically to your Downloads, with the story's name and the date in the file name. On phones, Share or save to phone sends it straight to your gallery, Messenger or another app, and Save again downloads another copy.

  • Videos are saved as MP4, which plays almost everywhere, including CapCut, Messenger and TVs.
  • On a few phones the app uses a slower, steadier way to make the video. It tells you when it does; just keep the screen open a little longer.

4. Share it

Send the video in your class or family group chat, show it on the classroom TV, or upload it to YouTube or Facebook. See Sharing your video and keeping your work.

Tip: Using a sample exactly as it is is perfectly fine. Change only what you want, like a child's name, and leave the rest.

Small changes that make it yours

  • Change a name. Swap the main character's name for your child's or your class's name. The AI voice will read it the new way.
  • Change the last scene. Add a line for your class, like "Thank you, Grade 2, for listening!"
  • Record the first line yourself. A parent's or teacher's real voice at the start makes children pay attention.
  • Pick different music. Under Whole story, try another track; it changes the mood a lot.

How long does it take?

Playing a ready-made story takes a minute or two. Making the video usually takes less time than the story itself on a computer, and a bit longer on an older phone. If you made changes to the words, making new AI voices takes up to a minute per scene the first time.
