Choosing pictures, characters and gentle movement

Telar Stories is designed to look like a picture book that talks: calm pictures, small movements and nothing flashy. A few simple choices make each scene clear and easy to watch.

Backgrounds

Open the Background tab and tap a place for the scene. Keep the same background for scenes that happen in the same place; change it when the story moves somewhere new. It helps young viewers follow the story.

Characters and poses

In the Characters tab, tap a pose to add it. Then tap the character in the picture to:

  • Move it by dragging.
  • Flip it to face the other way, for example toward another character.
  • Make it Smaller or Bigger, or Bring to front.
  • Remove it from the scene.

Two or three characters per scene is plenty. Put the character who is speaking a little bigger or nearer the middle.

Movement and light

  • Movement: gentle breathing for calm scenes, hop for happy moments, walk across when someone arrives or leaves, and steady for no motion at all.
  • Light: day, sunset or night, to match the background.
  • Camera: a slow zoom in draws attention to a moment, a slow zoom out works well at the end, and still keeps everything calm.

Tip: If a scene feels busy, remove a character or switch the camera to still. Simple scenes are easier for children to watch.

Characters you can use in any story

The Characters tab is grouped. Some groups belong to a ready-made story, like Noah's Ark, but every character can be used in your own stories too. For everyday stories, start with:

  • Mira & Mochi: a little girl and her cat, in many poses.
  • Mei's family: Mei, her mom and her dad, with poses for standing, waving, walking, hugging, reading and more.
  • Friends Around the World: five children and their teacher, Ms. Rose, for school stories.

Each pose is a separate picture. To show a character doing something new in the next scene, add the same character in a different pose, for example Mei · walk in one scene and Mei · wave in the next.

Backgrounds for every season

The Background tab has two groups. Story places are homes, schools, gardens and Bible settings. From Telar Clips are scenes from our free video library, good for seasons and celebrations: a snowy Christmas village, New Year fireworks, a crescent-moon town for Ramadan, temple steps with lamps for Diwali, green hills with a rainbow, an autumn park, a tropical beach and more.

Tip: A few backgrounds have water across the bottom, like the harbour and the starry lake. Use them for opening or closing scenes without characters, or for words like "The stars came out over the lake."

Making characters look right together

  • Keep sizes believable. Children should be shorter than adults. Use Smaller and Bigger until they look right standing together.
  • Put feet on the ground. Drag characters so their feet sit on a floor, path or grass, not in the sky.
  • Face each other when talking. Use Flip so two characters look at each other.
  • Match the light. Use sunset glow or night on characters in evening scenes, so they don't look too bright.
