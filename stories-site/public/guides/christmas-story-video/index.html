Make a Christmas story video for your family, class or church

A narrated Christmas story makes a warm greeting for family far away, a children's message for a Christmas service, or a closing activity before the school break.

Three kinds of Christmas story

  • The Nativity: The First Christmas is ready to play, from the angel's visit to Mary to the shepherds at the stable.
  • A kindness story: The Christmas Gift tells how Lily gives up a present to cheer up her lonely neighbor, Mr. Ben.
  • Your own family story: your children, your home, and how your family celebrates.

A family Christmas greeting in six scenes

  1. Title: "Christmas with the Santos Family" over the snowy Christmas village or the living room with fireplace.
  2. The children waiting for Christmas, counting the days.
  3. Getting ready: baking, decorating, wrapping gifts. Use Mei's gift pose or Lily's baking poses.
  4. Remembering the true meaning: a short line about Jesus, or about love and family.
  5. A message for the people watching: "We miss you, Lola and Lolo!"
  6. "Merry Christmas and a happy New Year!"

Backgrounds for the season

Under Story places: the snowy street, the cozy bedroom, the toy shop, the Christmas kitchen, the front door in the snow, the living room with fireplace, and the stable with the star. Under From Telar Clips: the snowy Christmas village and, for New Year, the fireworks over the harbour.

Narration and music

  • Let each family member record one scene with ● Record my voice. Children love hearing themselves in the video.
  • Choose gentle music, like Gentle: Soft piano or Warm and happy: Sunday.
  • Keep it under two minutes, so people watch to the end on their phones.

Sharing it

Send the MP4 in your family group chat, or post it on Facebook. For a church or school program, play the downloaded file on the projector. See Sharing your video and keeping your work.

Tip: Save a backup of your Christmas story. Next year, open it, change the names and the year, add the new baby or the new puppy, and make a fresh greeting in minutes.

Ideas for a church Christmas program

  • Play The First Christmas before the children's nativity play, so the audience knows the story.
  • Have the Sunday school class record the narration, one child per scene.
  • End the program with a short video greeting from the church, with the service times in the last scene.
