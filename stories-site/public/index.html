Make a story video in 4 steps

📖 Like a picture book that talks. No editing skills needed: pictures, gentle movements, captions and a friendly narrator. No complicated effects or big animations.

A sample story is already loaded. Use it as it is, or change anything you like. Need more help? Read the guides, or get free printables for each story.

📶 Tip: use a strong Wi-Fi or mobile data connection. The pictures, music and AI voice load faster, and the first visit downloads the most. After that, many things load from your device.

  1. Pick a scene in the strip under the picture. Add more with + Scene.
  2. Set the scene: open Background to choose a place and Characters to add poses. Drag characters to move them; tap one to flip, resize or remove it.
  3. Tell the story: in Words & voice, type what happens, then add a voice: a free AI storyteller voice or Record my voice. The title and words in the sample are just suggestions; mix and match.
  4. Press ▶ Play to watch it, then ⬇ Make video to save it to your device.
